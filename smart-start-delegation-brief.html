Task overview

What task or decision are you looking to delegate? Start by naming it clearly, then get clear on who's taking it on and when it needs to happen by. The more specific you are here, the easier every later step becomes.

Context helps people prioritise properly

What "done" looks like

Describe the finished result in concrete terms. What will you see, read, or receive when this is complete? Be specific, a vague definition creates a vague result.

Be clear about scope so nothing unexpected lands back on your desk

The standard required

This is where most delegation breaks down. Be honest about what level of quality this task actually needs.

Tone, format, brand guidelines, anything that matters to you
What have you had to fix before? Say it upfront.

Resources & context

Give them everything they need before they start, not after they get stuck.

Check-in points

Agree check-ins upfront. This removes the need to hover, and gives them confidence to move forward without you.

Enough to catch problems early, not so often it defeats the point of delegating

Before you send this

Share this brief with the person taking the task on, don't just keep it for your own reference. If they can't see what "done well" looks like or what they can decide without you, the handoff hasn't actually happened yet.
