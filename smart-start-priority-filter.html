What needs attention first?

The Priority Filter

Score and rank competing priorities to see what needs attention first.

Use this when you already know several issues, opportunities or possible investments and need to decide what deserves attention first. Add up to five, score each one, and see how they compare.

What this does not do: it does not diagnose where a bottleneck is coming from, and it does not tell you how to solve the priority you choose. The Bottleneck Finder and the Decision Tree can help with those.

Take your time with this. There are no right or wrong answers, just your honest read of the business today.
