What does a new person need to achieve?

The 3-Box Role Builder

Get clear on what the role needs to achieve before you write the job description.

Before you start with a job title or a list of tasks, step back and define why the role is needed, what the person should achieve and what they will own.

This should only take a few minutes. You can transfer your answers into the Job Description + Person Specification Template when you're ready.

Take your time with this. There are no right or wrong answers, just what this role genuinely needs to achieve.

What problem, opportunity or business need are you trying to address?

Keep this focused on the business need rather than the job title.

1 Outcomes

What needs to be different because this person is here?

Think about results rather than tasks. What are the 3 most important things this person should make happen?

Instead of: "Manage customer enquiries." Think: "Improve how customer enquiries are handled so they are responded to consistently and followed through."
2 Ownership

What will this person be expected to own?

Think about the areas they should take responsibility for, rather than every individual task they might do.

3 Success

How will you know the role is working?

What would you expect to see if this person was succeeding?

Examples: improved delivery, increased capacity, better customer outcomes, stronger commercial results, fewer delays, improved quality, clearer ownership, reduced pressure on the owner or manager.
