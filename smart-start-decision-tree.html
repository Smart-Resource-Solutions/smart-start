What's the smartest way to get the work done?

Work out whether to redesign the work, automate it, develop someone internally, outsource it, use flexible support or hire.

Come to this with one reasonably clear need in mind. Answer a short series of questions and this will suggest the most likely route, from stopping the work altogether through to recruiting.

What this does not do: it does not diagnose where a bottleneck is coming from. "Where is your business getting stuck?" can help with that first.

Take your time with this. There are no right or wrong answers, just what's realistic for this particular need.
