Why decisions, not just tasks: being the bottleneck usually isn't about having too much on your plate, it's about being the person everything still has to pass through before it can move. List decisions here too, not just the visible work.

When you log hours against a decision, count the time from when you were first asked to when you actually decided, not just the few minutes it took to think it through. A decision that took five minutes of actual thought but sat waiting for three days before you got to it is a three day bottleneck, log it as such.
