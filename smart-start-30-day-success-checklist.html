How do we give the new hire a strong start?

The 30-Day Success Checklist

Give your new hire a clear, structured first month.

Work through this from the moment someone accepts an offer through to a 30 day review. It does not cover deciding whether to hire, defining the role, advertising or interviewing, those come earlier.

Take your time with this. There are no right or wrong answers, just what this particular hire needs.

Personal details

Optional. Adding these personalises the checklist above and lets you assign section ownership by name below.

Every section and item here is editable. Rename a section, add or remove items, add your own section, and set who is responsible and a due date where it helps.

30-day review

Prompts for the manager to discuss with the new hire.

  • What's going well?
  • What's unclear?
  • What has been harder than expected?
  • What support is needed?
  • Are priorities still right?
  • What should the next 30 days focus on?
